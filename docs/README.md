# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Use this README as the starting point for understanding and navigating OctoAcme's project management documentation set. It provides a quick view of how teams move work from idea to release while maintaining quality, transparency, and continuous improvement.

OctoAcme's approach follows a practical lifecycle supported by shared artifacts and clear ownership. Teams validate a business need, define measurable outcomes, plan work as shippable increments, execute with regular tracking and quality gates, and release with readiness checks and rollback plans. Risks, dependencies, and stakeholder updates are managed continuously, and retrospective action items are fed back into planning to improve future delivery.

## Project management process summary

- **Initiation**: Confirm the business need, define success criteria, identify stakeholders, and produce a one-pager, initial milestones, and early risks before a go/no-go decision.
- **Planning**: Run kickoff, build a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimate work, define Definition of Done, map dependencies, and align milestones and release timelines.
- **Execution**: Deliver through daily/weekly team rhythm, small PRs, CI validation, reviews, and QA practices (unit, integration, and critical-flow smoke tests).
- **Release & deployment**: Ensure acceptance criteria, CI, and security checks are complete; prepare release notes and rollback plans; validate in staging and production; then communicate outcomes.
- **Risk management & communication**: Maintain a risk register, review risks regularly, use a single source of truth for status, and escalate blockers through the defined path when needed.
- **Retrospective & continuous improvement**: Hold retros after sprints, releases, and incidents; capture what worked and what did not; prioritize a few actions and track them to completion.
